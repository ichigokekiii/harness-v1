Food webs, Grade 7
How does the Grade 7 unit show energy moving through a food web?

The unit treats energy as a path, not a pile of names.

Grade 7 Ecosystems (sample) says a food web shows how energy moves.

Start from the sun, then the producer, then each eater. Keep one path on the board so Grade 7 does not mix it with a food chain.
