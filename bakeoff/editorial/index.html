Grade 7 Ecosystems

How does a food web move energy in the Grade 7 ecosystems unit?

Grade 7 Ecosystems (sample) says a food web shows how energy moves from producers through consumers in the living things unit. Arrows in the web point toward the organism that receives the energy.

Grade 7 Science Curriculum Guide, page 32. Current.
